# YouTube Packaging

1. Title videos as `[Topic] | [Level] English Listening`.
2. State the level, topic and LingRoot promise in the description.
3. Use both a topic playlist and the matching level playlist.
4. Use placeholders until cross-level video URLs exist.
5. Keep privacy `private` unless the explicit publish gates allow otherwise.
6. Validate metadata with `schemas/youtube-metadata.schema.json`.
7. Avoid keyword stuffing and unsupported educational claims.
