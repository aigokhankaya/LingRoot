- When displaying "LingRoot" brand name, use TWO COLORS:
  - "Ling" = White (#FFFFFF)
  - "Root" = Steel Blue (#5889A5)
- Example: Text "LingRoot" with "Ling" in white and "Root" in steel blue (#5889A5).
