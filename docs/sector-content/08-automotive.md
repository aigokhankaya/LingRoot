## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Araba Satın Alma

**Salesperson:** Welcome! Are you looking for anything specific today?

**Customer:** Yes, I'm interested in the new electric sedan. I've read good things about its range.

**Salesperson:** Excellent choice. The new Model E has a range of 400 miles. It also features autonomous driving capabilities.

**Customer:** That sounds impressive. What about charging?

**Salesperson:** It comes with a home charging station included in the price. Plus, you can use our supercharger network for free for the first year.

**Customer:** And the price?

**Salesperson:** The base model starts at $45,000. With federal tax credits, you can save around $7,500.

**Customer:** Can I take it for a test drive?

**Salesperson:** Absolutely. Let me get the keys.

### Servis Randevusu

**Advisor:** Service department, how can I help you?

**Customer:** Hi, my check engine light just came on and the car is vibrating at high speeds.

**Advisor:** I see. Does the light flash or stay solid?

**Customer:** It stays solid yellow.

**Advisor:** Okay, that usually indicates a sensor issue, but we need to run diagnostics. The vibration could be wheel alignment.

**Customer:** When can I bring it in?

**Advisor:** We have an opening tomorrow morning at 9 AM.

**Customer:** That works. How long will it take?

**Advisor:** The diagnostic takes about an hour. Once we know the problem, I can give you a time estimate for the repair.

**Customer:** Okay, see you tomorrow.
