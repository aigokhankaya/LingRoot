### Hesap Açma

**Customer:** Good morning. I'd like to open a savings account.

**Banker:** Of course. Do you have an account with us currently?

**Customer:** No, this would be my first account with your bank.

**Banker:** I see. We have several savings options. Are you looking for a regular savings account or a high-yield account?

**Customer:** What's the difference in interest rates?

**Banker:** Our regular savings offers 2.5% APY, while the high-yield account offers 4.0% APY with a minimum balance of $10,000.

**Customer:** I'll go with the high-yield account. What documents do I need?

**Banker:** You'll need a government-issued ID and proof of address. Also, we'll need your Social Security number for tax reporting.

**Customer:** I have everything here. How long does the process take?

**Banker:** Just about 15 minutes. Once we're done, you'll receive your debit card within 5-7 business days.

**Advisor:** Thank you for meeting with me today. Let's review your investment goals.

**Client:** I'm looking to invest $100,000 for retirement in about 20 years.

**Client:** That sounds reasonable. What are the fees?

**Client:** Let's proceed with this strategy. What's the next step?

**Advisor:** I'll prepare the investment policy statement. Once you sign, we can begin investing.
