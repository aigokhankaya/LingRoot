## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Ev Gösterimi

**Agent:** Here we are. This is the living room. As you can see, it gets a lot of natural light.

**Client:** It's very spacious. Are the floors hardwood?

**Agent:** Yes, they are original oak floors, recently refinished.

**Client:** Nice. How about the kitchen?

**Agent:** The kitchen was renovated last year. It has granite countertops and new stainless steel appliances.

**Client:** Is the dishwasher included?

**Agent:** Yes, all appliances stay with the house.

**Client:** What about the neighborhood? Is it noisy?

**Agent:** It's actually very quiet. Most neighbors are families. There is a park just two blocks away.

**Client:** I like it. I think I want to make an offer.

### Kira Pazarlığı

**Tenant:** I really like the apartment, but the rent is a bit higher than my budget.

**Landlord:** The price is competitive for this area. It includes water and internet.

**Tenant:** I understand. Would you be willing to lower it to $1,800 if I sign a two-year lease?

**Landlord:** Hmm, a two-year lease gives me more security...

**Tenant:** And I can pay three months in advance.

**Landlord:** Okay, if you pay three months upfront and sign for two years, I can accept $1,800.

**Tenant:** That sounds fair. When can I get the keys?

**Landlord:** As soon as the deposit clears and the contract is signed.
