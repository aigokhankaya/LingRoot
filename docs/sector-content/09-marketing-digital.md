## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Pazarlama Stratejisi Toplantısı

**Manager:** Okay team, let's discuss the Q4 strategy. Sales were flat in Q3.

**Marketer:** I think we need to shift our focus to digital channels. Our traditional print ads aren't performing.

**Manager:** Agreed. What do you suggest?

**Marketer:** We should invest heavily in content marketing and SEO. If we solve our customers' problems through blog posts, we build trust.

**Manager:** That's a good long-term strategy, but we need immediate sales.

**Marketer:** In that case, I recommend a PPC campaign on Google and LinkedIn targeting decision-makers.

**Manager:** How much budget do we need?

**Marketer:** Let's start with $5,000 a month and optimize based on the ROAS (Return on Ad Spend).

**Manager:** Sounds like a plan. Prepare a proposal by Friday.

### Sosyal Medya Krizi

**Community Manager:** Hey, we have a problem. A customer posted a negative review on Twitter and it's getting a lot of retweets.

**PR Manager:** What is the complaint about?

**Community Manager:** They claim our product arrived damaged and customer support ignored them.

**PR Manager:** We need to act fast. Do not delete the comment. Reply publicly.

**Community Manager:** What should I say?

**PR Manager:** Apologize for the experience, ask them to DM us their order number, and promise to resolve it immediately. Transparency is key here.

**Community Manager:** Okay, I'm posting the reply now.

**PR Manager:** Also, alert the support team to prioritize this ticket once it comes in.
