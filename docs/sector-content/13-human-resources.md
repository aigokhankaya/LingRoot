## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### İş Mülakatı

**Interviewer:** Good morning, thank you for coming. Please have a seat.

**Candidate:** Good morning. Thank you for inviting me.

**Interviewer:** I've reviewed your CV and I'm impressed with your experience in project management. Can you tell me about the last project you led?

**Candidate:** Certainly. I led a team of five developers to launch a mobile app. We finished two weeks ahead of schedule and under budget.

**Interviewer:** That's excellent. How do you handle conflict within your team?

**Candidate:** I believe in open communication. When two team members disagreed, I facilitated a meeting where everyone could voice their concerns, and we found a compromise.

**Interviewer:** Very good. Do you have any questions for me?

**Candidate:** Yes, what does a typical day look like for someone in this position?

**Interviewer:** It involves a daily stand-up meeting, coordinating with clients, and tracking progress.

### Performans Değerlendirmesi

**Manager:** Hi John, let's discuss your annual review.

**Employee:** Sure, I'm ready.

**Manager:** Overall, you've had a solid year. You met all your sales targets, which is great.

**Employee:** Thank you. I worked hard to close those deals in Q4.

**Manager:** However, I noticed some issues with your timely reporting. Several weekly reports were late.

**Employee:** You're right. I sometimes get caught up in client calls and forget the administrative work.

**Manager:** I need you to prioritize that next year. Accuracy is as important as speed.

**Employee:** I understand. I will set reminders to ensure they are done on time.

**Manager:** Good. If you improve that, you'll be on track for a promotion.
