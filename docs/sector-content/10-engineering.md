## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Tasarım İncelemesi

**Engineer A:** Let's look at the prototype for the new valve.

**Engineer B:** Overall, it looks good, but I'm concerned about the seal material. It might not withstand high temperatures.

**Engineer A:** The specs say it's rated for 200 degrees Celsius.

**Engineer B:** Yes, but in our application, the temperature can spike to 250 degrees. We should use a Teflon seal instead of rubber.

**Engineer A:** That will increase the cost.

**Engineer B:** True, but it will prevent failure and warranty claims later. It's a necessary trade-off.

**Engineer A:** Agreed. I'll update the bill of materials and the CAD drawing.

### Şantiye Sorunu

**Site Manager:** Why has the excavation stopped?

**Foreman:** We hit a water pipe that wasn't on the drawings. It's leaking badly.

**Site Manager:** Did you shut off the main valve?

**Foreman:** Yes, the water is off now, but the trench is flooded. We need to pump it out.

**Site Manager:** Okay, get the submersible pump running. I'll call the utility company to report the damage.

**Foreman:** Should we send the excavators to the other side of the site?

**Site Manager:** Yes, let's not waste time. Move them to Zone B until this is resolved.
