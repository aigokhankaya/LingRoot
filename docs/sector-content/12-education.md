## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Ofis Saati (Office Hours)

**Student:** Excuse me, Professor. Do you have a moment?

**Professor:** Sure, come in. How can I help you?

**Student:** I'm having trouble with the concept of quantum entanglement we discussed in class yesterday.

**Professor:** It is a difficult concept. Which part specifically is confusing you?

**Student:** I understand the basic idea, but I don't get the mathematical proof on page 42.

**Professor:** Okay, let's look at it together. The equation relies on the assumption that the two particles are in a superposition state.

**Student:** Ah, I missed that assumption. That explains why my calculation was wrong.

**Professor:** Exactly. I recommend reading the supplementary chapter I grabbed on the portal. It explains it in more detail.

**Student:** I will. Thank you, Professor.

### Grup Çalışması

**Alex:** We need to finish the group project by Friday.

**Sara:** I know. We still need to write the introduction and the conclusion.

**Mike:** I can write the introduction. I've done the background research.

**Alex:** Great. I'll focus on analyzing the survey data and creating the charts.

**Sara:** That leaves the conclusion for me. I'll also proofread the whole document before we submit it.

**Mike:** Should we meet tomorrow to put everything together?

**Alex:** Yes, let's meet in the library at 4 PM.

**Sara:** Sounds good. See you then.

```

Dear Professor [Last Name],

I hope you are having a good week.

I am working on Assignment 2 for your [Course Name] class, and I have a question regarding the second problem.

The instructions mention using the "Smith Method," but I couldn't find a reference to it in the textbook. Could you please clarify if we should use the lecture notes for this?

Thank you for your time and guidance.

Sincerely,
[Student Name]
[Student ID]
```
