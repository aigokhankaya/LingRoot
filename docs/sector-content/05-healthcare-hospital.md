## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Doktora Gitmek

**Doctor:** Good morning, take a seat. What brings you here today?

**Patient:** Good morning, Doctor. I've been having a terrible headache for three days and I feel dizzy.

**Doctor:** I see. Do you have any other symptoms? Nausea or fever?

**Patient:** Yes, I feel a bit nauseous, but I don't think I have a fever.

**Doctor:** Okay, let me check your blood pressure... It's a bit high - 140 over 90. Have you been under a lot of stress lately?

**Patient:** Yes, work has been very stressful this month.

**Doctor:** Stress can certainly cause tension headaches and elevate blood pressure. I'm going to prescribe a mild painkiller and something to help you relax.

**Patient:** Thank you, Doctor. Do I need to take any time off work?

**Doctor:** I recommend taking at least two days off to rest. Drink plenty of water and try to sleep early.

**Patient:** I will. Thank you.

### Eczanede

**Pharmacist:** Hello, how can I help you?

**Customer:** Hi, I'd like to pick up a prescription for Sarah Jenkins.

**Pharmacist:** Let me check... Yes, here it is. Antibiotics and pain relief.

**Customer:** What is the dosage instructions?

**Pharmacist:** For the antibiotic, take one tablet every 12 hours. It's important to finish the whole box, even if you feel better.

**Customer:** Okay, and the painkiller?

**Pharmacist:** Take one pill every 6 hours as needed for pain. Take it with food to avoid an upset stomach.

**Customer:** Got it. Are there any side effects?

**Pharmacist:** The antibiotic might cause mild drowsiness, so be careful if you're driving.

**Customer:** Thank you very much.
