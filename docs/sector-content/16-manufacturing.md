## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Sorun Giderme

**Operator:** Supervisor, we have a problem on Line 2.

**Supervisor:** What is it?

**Operator:** The packaging machine keeps jamming. It's tearing the boxes.

**Supervisor:** Did you check the tension settings?

**Operator:** Yes, I adjusted them, but it's still happening. I think the cutting blade is dull.

**Supervisor:** Okay, stop the line. I'll call maintenance to replace the blade. How much downtime will this cause?

**Operator:** It should only take about 15 minutes.

**Supervisor:** Alright, log the incident and clean the area while we wait.

### Üretim Toplantısı

**Manager:** Let's look at last week's numbers. We missed the target by 5%. Why?

**Team Lead:** We had an unexpected power outage on Wednesday that lasted 4 hours. That killed our momentum.

**Manager:** I see. Apart from that, how was the efficiency?

**Team Lead:** Efficiency was actually up. The new automated feeder is working well.

**Manager:** Good. Can we make up the lost production this weekend?

**Team Lead:** Yes, if we run a Saturday shift, we can catch up. The team is willing to work overtime.

**Manager:** Approved. Schedule the overtime and ensure we hit the monthly quota.
