## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Paket Sorgulama

**Patient:** Hello, I'm interested in getting dental implants in Turkey. Can you give me some information?

**Coordinator:** Certainly! We are a top-rated dental clinic in Istanbul. Our implant package includes the surgery, 5-star hotel accommodation, and VIP transfers.

**Patient:** That sounds good. How much does a full set cost?

**Coordinator:** A full mouth restoration starts from €5,000, but we need to see your X-ray to give an exact quote. It's about 70% cheaper than in the UK.

**Patient:** Wow, that's a big difference. Is the quality same?

**Coordinator:** Absolutely. We use FDA-approved Swiss and German implant brands, and our dentists have over 15 years of experience. We also offer a lifetime warranty.

**Patient:** And how long does it take?

**Coordinator:** You will need two visits. The first visit is for the implants (3-4 days), and the second visit is for the crowns (5-6 days) after 3 months of healing.

**Patient:** Okay, I will send my X-ray today.

### Ameliyat Öncesi Bilgilendirme

**Surgeon:** Welcome. I've reviewed your photos, but I wanted to examine you in person.

**Patient:** Thank you, Doctor. I'm a bit nervous about the rhinoplasty.

**Surgeon:** That's completely normal. We will use the open technique to refine the tip and remove the hump. It will look very natural.

**Patient:** Will there be a lot of pain and bruising?

**Surgeon:** Discomfort is minimal. You will have some swelling and bruising around the eyes for 3-4 days, but it fades quickly.

**Patient:** When can I go out?

**Surgeon:** You should rest at the hotel for the first 2 days. Afterward, you can explore the city, but avoid strenuous activity. We will remove the splint on the 6th day.

**Patient:** Sounds good. I'm ready.

**Surgeon:** Great. My assistant will guide you for the pre-op blood tests now.
