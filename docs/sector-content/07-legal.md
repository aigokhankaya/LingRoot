## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Sözleşme İncelemesi

**Client:** Have you had a chance to look at the vendor contract?

**Lawyer:** Yes, I've reviewed it. There are a few clauses that concern me.

**Client:** Which ones?

**Lawyer:** Specifically, the indemnity clause is too broad. It puts too much liability on your company if something goes wrong.

**Client:** What do you suggest?

**Lawyer:** We should request to limit the liability to the value of the contract. Also, the termination notice period is too long—6 months.

**Client:** I agree. 30 days is standard for us.

**Lawyer:** I will mark up the contract with these changes and send it back to their counsel for review.

**Client:** Thank you. Let's hope they accept the revisions.

### Mahkeme Çıkışı

**Journalist:** Do you have any comment on the verdict?

**Attorney:** My client is pleased with the jury's decision. Justice has been served today.

**Journalist:** The prosecution mentioned they might appeal. Are you worried?

**Attorney:** We are confident that the verdict will stand. The evidence clearly showed that my client acted in self-defense.

**Journalist:** What is the next step for your client?

**Attorney:** He looks forward to returning to his normal life and putting this behind him. We have no further comments at this time. Thank you.

## 📧 E-posta Şablonları

### Cease and Desist (Durdurma İhtarı)

```
Subject: CEASE AND DESIST: Copyright Infringement

Dear [Name],

We represent [Client Name], the owner of the copyright for [Work Name].

It has come to our attention that you are using our client's intellectual property on your website [URL] without authorization. This constitutes copyright infringement under applicable laws.

We demand that you:
1. Immediately remove all infringing content from your website.
2. Cease and desist from any further use of our client's work.
3. Confirm in writing that you have complied with this request within 48 hours.

Failure to comply will result in legal action seeking damages and injunctive relief.

This letter is sent without prejudice to our client's rights.

Sincerely,
[Attorney Name]
[Law Firm]
```

### Sözleşme Revizyonu Gönderimi

```
Subject: Re: Service Agreement Draft - Comments

Dear Mr. Smith,

Thank you for sending the draft Service Agreement.

We have reviewed the document and have attached a redline version with our proposed changes. 

Key revisions include:
- Limitation of Liability: Capped at $50,000.
- Jurisdiction: Changed to the State of New York.
- Payment Terms: Adjusted to Net 30 days.

We believe these changes reflect a more balanced approach for both parties.

Please let us know if these revisions are acceptable so we can proceed to the final version.

Best regards,
[Name]
General Counsel
```
