## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Pilot ve Kule (ATC)

**Pilot:** Tower, Delta 592, ready for takeoff, runway 27 Right.

**Tower:** Delta 592, hold short of runway 27 Right, traffic landing.

**Pilot:** Holding short, 27 Right, Delta 592.

**(Pause)**

**Tower:** Delta 592, wind 250 at 10 knots, runway 27 Right, cleared for takeoff.

**Pilot:** Cleared for takeoff, 27 Right, Delta 592.

**Tower:** Delta 592, contact departure on 124.5. Good day.

**Pilot:** 124.5, Delta 592. Good day.

### Check-in Bankosu

**Agent:** Good morning, where are you flying to today?

**Passenger:** Flying to London Heathrow.

**Agent:** May I have your passport and ticket, please?

**Passenger:** Here you go.

**Agent:** Are you checking any bags?

**Passenger:** Just one suitcase. And I have a carry-on.

**Agent:** Okay, place the suitcase on the scale... It's 24 kilos. The limit is 23. You'll have to pay an excess baggage fee or remove some items.

**Passenger:** Oh, I'll remove a book and put it in my carry-on.

**Agent:** (After adjustment) That's fine now. Here is your boarding pass. Gate B12. Boarding starts at 10:30.
