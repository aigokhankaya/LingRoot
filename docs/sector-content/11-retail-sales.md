## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Ayakkabı Mağazasında

**Customer:** Excuse me, do you have these sneakers in size 10?

**Sales Assistant:** Let me check the stockroom. One moment please... Good news, we have one pair left in size 10.

**Customer:** Great, can I try them on?

**Sales Assistant:** Sure, take a seat here. How do they feel?

**Customer:** They feel a bit tight around the toes.

**Sales Assistant:** They might stretch a little. Or would you like to try a half size bigger?

**Customer:** Yes, let's try the 10.5.

**Sales Assistant:** Here you go.

**Customer:** These are much better. Ill take them.

**Sales Assistant:** Excellent choice. Would you like some shoe protector spray with that? It keeps them clean longer.

**Customer:** No thanks, just the shoes.

### B2B Satış Görüşmesi

**Sales Rep:** Thank you for meeting me. I know your company is looking to upgrade its software.

**Client:** Yes, our current system is too slow. We need something faster and scalable.

**Sales Rep:** Our solution is cloud-based and designed for speed. It can handle double your current transaction volume without any lag.

**Client:** That sounds good, but what about the cost?

**Sales Rep:** It's a subscription model. Although the monthly fee is higher than your current maintenance costs, you save on server hardware and IT support staff.

**Client:** Can you show me a detailed cost comparison?

**Sales Rep:** Absolutely. I have prepared a ROI analysis here. As you can see, you will break even in 6 months.

**Client:** impressive. Leave the proposal with me, and I'll discuss it with the board.
