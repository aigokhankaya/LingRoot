## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Otele Giriş (Check-in)

**Receptionist:** Good afternoon, welcome to the Grand Hotel. How may I help you?

**Guest:** Hi, I have a reservation for three nights. The name is Sarah Connor.

**Receptionist:** Let me check... Yes, I have your reservation for a Deluxe Room from today until Friday. May I have your passport and credit card, please?

**Guest:** Sure, here they are.

**Receptionist:** Thank you. I just need you to sign this registration form here.

**Guest:** Is breakfast included in the booking?

**Receptionist:** Yes, breakfast is served daily from 7:00 to 10:30 AM in the main restaurant on the ground floor.

**Guest:** Great. Also, do you have Wi-Fi?

**Receptionist:** Yes, it’s free for all guests. The password is "GrandGuest2026". Here is your key card. You are in room 405. Take the elevator to the 4th floor and turn left.

**Guest:** Thank you very much.

**Receptionist:** My pleasure. Enjoy your stay!

### Şikayet Yönetimi

**Guest:** Excuse me, I have a problem with my room.

**Receptionist:** I'm sorry to hear that, sir. What seems to be the issue?

**Guest:** The air conditioning isn't working, and the room is extremely hot.

**Receptionist:** I apologize for the inconvenience. Let me contact our maintenance team immediately to check it.

**Guest:** How long will it take? I experienced a long flight and just want to rest.

**Receptionist:** I understand completely. If they can't fix it within 10 minutes, we will move you to another room.

**Guest:** That would be appreciated.

**Receptionist:** In the meantime, please accept these vouchers for a complimentary drink at the bar while you wait.

**Guest:** Thank you, that's very kind.
