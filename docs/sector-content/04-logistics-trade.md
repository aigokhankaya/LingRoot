## 🎯 Diyalog Örnekleri

### Nakliye Teklifi İsteme

**Logistics Manager:** Hello, I'd like to get a quote for a shipment to Hamburg, Germany.

**Freight Forwarder:** Certainly. Is this for air freight or sea freight?

**Logistics Manager:** Sea freight. We have two 40-foot containers ready for pickup.

**Freight Forwarder:** Okay. What is the commodity and the total weight?

**Logistics Manager:** It's automotive parts, non-hazardous. Total weight is approximately 40 tons.

**Freight Forwarder:** Understood. Do you require door-to-door delivery or port-to-port?

**Logistics Manager:** Port-to-port is fine. We will handle the inland transport in Germany.

**Freight Forwarder:** Great. The estimated transit time is 25 days. I'll email you the quote including terminal handling charges within an hour.

### Gümrük Sorunu

**Importer:** Hi, has our shipment cleared customs yet?

**Broker:** There's a slight delay. The customs officer requested additional documentation regarding the value of the goods.

**Importer:** What specifically do they need? We provided the commercial invoice.

**Broker:** They want proof of payment to verify the declared value matches the transaction amount.

**Importer:** I see. I can send the bank transfer confirmation immediately.

**Broker:** Perfect. Once I receive that, I'll resubmit it to processing. It should be released within 24 hours after that.

**Importer:** Please keep me updated. We urgently need these raw materials for production.
